# LSO KALIBER
**Universitas Muhammadiyah Malang**

  

LSO KALIBER is a student organization focused on **cyber security, technology, and continuous learning**. We provide a collaborative environment for students to develop technical skills, explore cybersecurity disciplines, build projects, and participate in competitive activities.


  

## Our Focus

  

We focus on developing practical skills and knowledge across various areas of cybersecurity through:

  

- Technical learning and hands-on practice

- Cybersecurity research and exploration

- Capture The Flag (CTF) and cybersecurity competitions

- Internal training and knowledge sharing

- Collaborative projects and experimentation

- Community development and networking

## Our Community

  

LSO KALIBER is more than a place to learn cybersecurity. We believe that strong technical skills grow faster through collaboration, experimentation, and shared knowledge.

  

Whether you're learning your first command-line tool or already breaking things that probably should not be broken, there is always something to learn.
